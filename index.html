Economics & Development · Research

Infrastructure as an instrument of power

Author: Global Inquiry Forum Editorial Desk · Type: Research · Status: Coming soon · Field: Economics & Development

Examining how trade, connectivity, investment and technology influence global political economy.

Keywords: infrastructure · connectivity · investment · trade · political economy

Research approach

The forum will treat infrastructure as a multidimensional subject, examining economic effects alongside institutions, connectivity, strategic interests and distributional consequences.

Evidence standards

Research pieces should identify their evidence base and distinguish empirical observations from analytical interpretation. Quantitative claims should be traceable to their underlying data where possible.

Research transparencyAuthors may be asked to provide datasets, coding notes, interview methods or supplementary material where appropriate and ethically permissible.

Author: Global Inquiry Forum Editorial Desk · Type: Perspective · Status: Coming soon · Field: AI & Technology

Exploring technological change, diplomacy, governance and strategic competition.

Keywords: artificial intelligence · diplomacy · governance · technology · statecraft

Scope

This research stream will examine AI governance, diplomacy, technological competition and the changing relationship between technology and international affairs.

Responsible publication

AI-assisted work remains subject to human authorship, source verification and editorial accountability. Meaningful use of generative AI should be disclosed where required by the forum's policy.

Author responsibilityAuthors remain responsible for factual accuracy, originality, attribution and the integrity of their submitted work.

About the forum

Research first. Perspectives welcome.

Global Inquiry Forum is being developed as an independent digital platform for interdisciplinary research and public-facing scholarship. Its purpose is to make serious ideas easier to discover, read and discuss.

The platform is designed around the strengths of established research institutions: clear research outputs, identifiable authors, thematic discovery, events and accessible public scholarship.

Evidence & attributionSources and author responsibility are central to published work.
Editorial qualitySubmissions will be reviewed before publication.
Interdisciplinary opennessIdeas are welcomed across fields and regions.
Author visibilityContributors build a lasting public research portfolio.

Contributors & internships

The forum can become a practical research community for students, early-career scholars and independent researchers.

PATH 01

Research contributor

Submit an original article, research note, review, interview or evidence-based analysis. Published contributors receive a visible author profile and publication record.

PATH 02

Research intern

Support literature discovery, source verification, data collection, bibliographies, visual research or public-facing research production under defined responsibilities.

PATH 03

Collaborating researcher

Develop a joint project, thematic series, interview project, dataset or event with the forum and its wider contributor network.

Editorial framework

How publication works

A transparent process will help contributors understand what happens between an idea and publication.

01 · SubmitSend an original piece with author details, sources and a short abstract.
02 · Editorial reviewEditors assess fit, evidence, originality, clarity and basic research integrity.
03 · RevisionWhere appropriate, contributors receive focused editorial feedback before publication.
04 · PublishApproved work is published with clear authorship, date, category and attribution.
AI-use disclosureAuthors remain responsible for claims and sources; meaningful AI assistance should be disclosed.
Research integrityPlagiarism, fabricated evidence, undisclosed conflicts and misleading attribution are not acceptable.
